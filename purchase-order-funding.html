WHAT IS PURCHASE ORDER FUNDING?

Funding To Help Businesses Fulfil Purchase Orders

Purchase order funding is a form of business funding that can help qualifying businesses obtain the capital required to fulfil an approved purchase order.

Instead of a business having to fund the full cost of goods or services from its own working capital, approved funding can be used to pay the relevant supplier.

Elysian Capital Investments provides purchase order funding solutions ranging from R50 000 to R1 Million, subject to approval and the required documentation.

HOW IT WORKS

Our Purchase Order Funding Process

01. Submit Your Purchase Order

Submit your purchase order or appointment letter for consideration.

02. Assessment

The submitted information and supporting documentation are considered for funding approval.

03. Supplier Payment

Approved funding is paid directly to the relevant supplier.

04. Cession

A cession with the client or bank is signed as security.

05. Approval

Approval can be completed within 24 hours once the required documentation has been received.
